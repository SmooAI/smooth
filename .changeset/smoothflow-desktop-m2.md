SmoothFlow Desktop M2 (th-9fd86d): tabs and splits of live terminals, with measured cell metrics, a block or hollow cursor, and resizing on layout. It adds the New Session sheet (a harness picker with degraded and not-installed rows, a repo-searching Directory field, inferred context and a prompt), Allow/Deny for permission requests, and the Linux/Windows keymap with `keybindings.toml` overrides. `smooth-flow-client` gains the keymap, harness picker, approvability and tab-strip rules, with four new conformance vector files.
