SmoothFlow Desktop's Diff tab scrolls long lines sideways instead of clipping them (wheel, trackpad, shift+wheel, or ←/→), with the line-number gutters pinned and both side-by-side panes moving together. Review comments are now multi-line: Enter adds a line, Ctrl/⌘+Enter adds the comment, and the box grows to six lines before scrolling with the caret (th-4ec2f6).
