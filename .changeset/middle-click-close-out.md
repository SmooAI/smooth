SmoothFlow: middle-click a session in the fleet sidebar to Close Out (th-f958f2). On the Mac app and SmoothFlow Desktop it opens the same Close Out confirmation as Close Out… and ⌘⌥W / Ctrl+Alt+W, and an engine refusal (a dirty or unmerged worktree) is shown verbatim, never forced. SmoothFlow Desktop gains Close Out itself: the confirmation names the kill, pearl, worktree and branch, then sends `flow.close`.
